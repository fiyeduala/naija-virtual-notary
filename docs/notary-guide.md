# Naija Virtual Notary — Guide for Notary Partners

*Everything you need to take your first booking, and everything the platform does on your behalf.*

---

## 1. What this platform is

Naija Virtual Notary connects clients who need a document notarised with practising notaries public who can do it remotely.

A client uploads their document and a means of identification, chooses a notary from the marketplace, and pays before anything reaches you. You verify their identity, place your signature, stamp and seal on the document, and the platform produces the finished, sealed PDF and delivers it to the client.

You keep your own prices, your own seal and your own decision on every job. The platform handles the client, the money and the paperwork around it.

**One thing worth saying plainly:** a request only ever appears on your desk after the client has paid. You are never asked to do work on a promise of payment.

## 2. Joining

### Step 1 — Apply

Go to **naijavirtualnotary.mtouchlegal.com/partner-with-us** and complete the partner application. This creates your account at the same time, so you will choose a password here.

### Step 2 — Confirm your email address

We send a short numeric code to the address you registered. Enter it on the screen that follows. If it does not arrive within a minute or two, check your spam folder and use the **Resend** button.

### Step 3 — Pay the one-time onboarding fee

Once your email is confirmed you will be shown the onboarding fee and a **Pay** button, which takes you to Paystack. The amount is displayed on the page; it is paid once, not per job, and it activates your application for review.

Payment can be made by card, bank transfer or USSD — whatever Paystack offers you at checkout.

### Step 4 — We review your application

An administrator checks your application and your credentials. You will be told by email when a decision is made. If something is missing, we will tell you what it is rather than simply declining.

### Step 5 — Complete your profile

After approval, sign in and go to **Profile**. Three things must be in place before clients can find you.

## 3. Completing your profile

### Your notarial assets

These are the marks that will appear on every document you notarise, so they are worth getting right once.

| What we need | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Signature** | A clear image of your signature |
| **Stamp** | Your notarial stamp |
| **Seal** | Your seal |
| **Initials** | Typed, not uploaded — a short form such as *A.O.* |
| **Supreme Court Number** | Optional, but it appears on your public profile and reassures clients |

**How to get a good image.** Sign or stamp on plain white paper, photograph it in daylight, and crop tightly. A PNG with a transparent background is ideal because it sits cleanly over the client's text; a JPG on white also works. Each file must be **PNG or JPG and under 4 MB**.

Your signature, stamp and seal are stored privately. They are never on a public web address, never attached to an email, and cannot be viewed by clients or by other notaries — only by you, by an administrator, and by the software at the moment it seals a document.

### Your bank details

Choose your bank from the list, then enter your **10-digit account number** and the account name.

We check the account with your bank as you save it, and tell you what your bank said:

- **Confirmed** — you are set up for payouts, nothing else to do.
- **Name does not match your profile** — perfectly normal for a chambers or company account. An administrator will simply confirm it before your first payout.
- **Could not be confirmed just now** — usually a temporary problem at the bank's end. Your details are saved and an administrator will verify them before you are paid.

Choose the bank from the dropdown rather than typing a name. A typed name cannot be paid to.

### Your services and prices

Add at least one service. For each one you set:

- **Service type** — for example an affidavit, a power of attorney, academic credentials, immigration documents
- **Price in naira** and **price in dollars** — set independently, so you are not at the mercy of an exchange rate
- **How long it usually takes**, in minutes, which is what fills your calendar slots
- **A short description**, optional but useful — clients read these when choosing

You can add as many services as you offer, and change prices whenever you like.

### Go live

Once you have all three notarial assets, a bank account and at least one service, press **Go live**. Your profile appears in the marketplace and clients can book you from that moment.

If the button refuses, it is telling you one of the three is still incomplete — most often the seal, which is easy to miss when the signature and stamp went up together.

> **Joining us from the old website?** Your account, your history and whatever we already held for you have been carried across. In most cases an administrator will complete your assets and bank details for you from the details you send us, and then switch your listing on. You do not need to re-apply or pay the onboarding fee again.

## 4. Taking a booking

### Your dashboard

Signing in brings you to your desk, which shows:

- **Requests waiting for your answer**, oldest first — the one at the top has been waiting longest
- **Jobs in progress**
- **What you have earned**: total, your share after commission, this month, what you are owed and what has already been paid to you
- **How you are doing**: work received over the last 30 days, your busiest days and hours, and how often you accept

### Accepting or declining

Open a request and you can see the client, the service they chose, the document they uploaded and the identification they provided — before you commit to anything.

- **Accept** — the job is yours and the session is scheduled.
- **Decline** — tell us why if you can. The request is passed on so the client is not left waiting. Declining costs you nothing; taking a job you cannot do costs the client their day.

### The response window

The platform gives you a response window — an agreed number of minutes to answer. When it passes, nothing is taken away from you automatically. The request simply shows as overdue on the administrator's desk, and they may step in and complete it so the client is not stranded.

**If that happens, two things stay true:**

1. The document is still sealed with **your** signature, stamp and seal, at **your** price. You remain the notary of record.
2. You still receive **your** agreed share of the fee.

Covering a job moves the keystrokes, not the money or the responsibility. It exists so a client never sits waiting on a document they have already paid for.

## 5. Doing the work

### Verifying the client's identity

Two ways, and you choose:

**A live video call.** Open the session and speak to the client face to face. The call is **not recorded**. You see them, you see their ID, you satisfy yourself it is the same person.

**The uploaded ID alone.** If the document and identification are straightforward and a call adds nothing, use **Skip the call** and verify from what they uploaded.

Either way, the platform writes a verification record: who verified, who was verified, which identity document was relied on, which method was used, the exact time, and the network address it happened from. That record is permanent and is part of the evidence behind the notarisation.

### Placing your signature, stamp and seal

The editor shows the client's document with your assets down the side. Drag each one onto the page where it belongs, resize it, and move between pages as needed. You can place your signature, stamp, seal, initials, the date and free text.

Then — and this matters — press **Save placements** before you finalise. The platform will not let you seal a document with nothing on it, but it cannot rescue placements that were never saved.

### Finalising

**Finalise** produces the sealed PDF, marks the job complete, and emails the client to tell them their document is ready. They download it from their own dashboard.

Once finalised, the document is fixed. Check your placements before you press it.

### Talking to the client

Every request has its own message thread, so questions and answers stay attached to the job they belong to rather than scattered across WhatsApp. Administrators can see these threads and can join in if a job needs help.

## 6. Getting paid

The client pays the platform up front. Your share is your price less the agreed commission, which is shown on your dashboard.

The moment a job completes, your share moves into **owed**. Payouts are then made by the platform, either by bank transfer to the account on your profile or through Paystack directly. Your dashboard always shows two separate figures — what you are **owed** and what has been **paid out** — and the gap between them is what is still coming to you.

If a transfer fails for any reason, the amount returns to *owed* rather than disappearing. Nothing is ever written off silently.

---

## 7. Keeping your details right

Your name, prices, services and notarial assets all appear to clients, so they need to be current.

You can update your **prices and services** yourself at any time.

For **your name, your assets, your bank account and your professional details**, contact an administrator. Changes to these are applied on your behalf so that nobody's seal or bank account can be altered without a second pair of eyes. In practice this is a quick email, not a formal process.

## 8. Common questions

**Do I need to be at a computer all day?**
No. Answer requests when you can. The response window exists so clients are not left in silence, not to keep you at a desk.

**What if I am away for a period?**
Tell an administrator and your listing can be switched off until you return. Existing bookings will be covered.

**Can clients see my signature or seal?**
Only where they belong — on a document you have notarised for them. The image files themselves are private.

**Can another notary use my seal?**
No. The editor only ever offers a notary their own assets, and the assets of a partner whose job an administrator is covering.

**What if the client's document is not something I can notarise?**
Decline it. That is what declining is for, and it is passed on immediately.

**What if a client disputes something later?**
Every step is recorded — payment, acceptance, identity verification, each placement, finalisation, with times and network addresses throughout. Contact an administrator and the full history can be produced.

## 9. Getting help

Use the live chat button on the site, the message thread on the job itself, or write to the platform administrators. If something is wrong with a document that has already been sealed, say so straight away rather than starting again — the original still exists and can be looked at.
